About Us

About Geeta Home Care

Geeta Home Care is a home healthcare service focused on providing reliable and professional medical care at the comfort of your home.

From a quick doctor visit to daily nursing after a hospital stay, our team brings clinical care to your home with the same standards as Geeta Clinic. Patients recover where they are most comfortable, and families stay close throughout.

  • Doctors and nurses who know home care
  • Visits planned around your family’s schedule
  • Service across all areas of Ahmedabad
